2. What this site does and does not collect

tricombinator.com is a static marketing and product-information site. We do not run advertising trackers, social pixels, or third-party analytics cookies on this site. We do not sell personal data.

2.1 Contact forms (personal data you submit)

When you use a contact form, you may provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company name (optional)
  • Message content and any other details you choose to include

Purpose: to respond to your inquiry, evaluate a possible business relationship, and keep a record of correspondence.

Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6): our legitimate interests in handling business inquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)), and where relevant steps prior to entering a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)). You are not obliged to submit a form; if you do not provide contact details we cannot reply.

How it is transmitted and stored: form submissions are sent to our email via the Web3Forms service (web3forms.com), which acts as a data processor. Message content is then handled in our business email. We do not build marketing profiles from form fields.

Retention: inquiry messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation and ordinary business records — typically up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact, unless a longer period is required for an ongoing relationship, legal claim, or statutory bookkeeping obligation.

2.2 Direct email or phone contact

If you write to business@tricombinator.com or call +358 45 125 8550, we process the contact details and content you provide for the same purposes and on the same legal bases as above.

2.3 Server and hosting logs (technical data)

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may automatically process technical data such as IP address, request time, URL, user agent, and similar connection metadata in server logs for security, abuse prevention, and service operation. We do not use those logs for advertising. Retention of raw logs is controlled by the host according to its own infrastructure practices.

Legal basis: legitimate interests in operating a secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

2.4 Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not set non-essential cookies for analytics or marketing. No cookie consent banner is shown because we do not load optional tracking technologies. If that changes, we will update this policy and obtain consent where required before non-essential cookies are stored.

Your browser may still store ordinary technical state needed to load the page. You can clear site data in your browser settings at any time.

2.5 Public historical replay pages

Pages under /replay/ show historical energy forecast illustrations on fixed past data. They do not collect account credentials or trading personal data from visitors beyond ordinary hosting logs described above.

3. Recipients and transfers

Personal data from contact forms may be processed by:

  • Tricombinator Oy personnel who handle business inquiries
  • Web3Forms — form delivery processor (privacy information)
  • GitHub / GitHub Pages — website hosting (privacy statement)
  • Email infrastructure used to receive and store business mail

Some processors may process data outside the EEA. Where that occurs, we rely on appropriate safeguards under GDPR Chapter V (for example Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision) as provided by those services. We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

4. Security

The public site is served over HTTPS. Access to business email and form notifications is limited to people who need it for their role. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a personal-data breach that requires notification, we will act as required by GDPR Articles 33–34.

To exercise these rights, email business@tricombinator.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We aim to reply within one month as required by the GDPR.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto):

  • Website: https://tietosuoja.fi/en/home
